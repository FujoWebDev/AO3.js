Series Updated:

Notes:

I gather and share fandom data for fun! Among other things, this series includes:

  • Biggest/most active fandoms over time (also a bunch about biggest ships and characters)

  • Shipping-related stats – including femslash, polyamory, asexuality and aromanticism, and gen, as well a look at the similarities and differences between F/F, F/M, and M/M

  • Gender stats - including Trans/NB stats & movies vs. fanworks

  • Trope stats – including AU stats and Omegaverse stats

  • Cross-platform comparisons (Wattpad, FFNet, AO3, etc)

  • Fandom deep dives – Sherlockian and otherwise, including how TV fandom fix-its relate to canon events across many fandoms

  • Misc – popularity, ratings, warnings, languages, completion

  • Reference – Fandom stats talks and tutorials

    • Note that everything is in roughly chronological order (except where I've made mistakes), so you'll want to go to the end of the series to see my most recent stuff.
